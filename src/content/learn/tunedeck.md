Open the Tunedeck from Now Playing (or View → Tunedeck). It docks from the right and pushes the rest of the app aside rather than covering it, so you can keep browsing with it open.

<!-- shot: tunedeck-track -->

It has four tabs, one per question: who made this, what this file is, what else is like it, and what's happening.

## Artist

This tab is about who you're hearing. It has a biography from Wikipedia and links out to the web. **Members** comes from MusicBrainz and lists current and former members, the bands they're in, side projects, collaborations, and aliases, including artists you don't have in your library. The ones you do have show how many of their tracks you own, and you can click through to them. Below that, **In your library** has **More by this artist** and **Appears on**, followed by your favorite songs by them and your listening totals. If the header names the wrong artist, use **Not this artist?** to correct it and Oscine remembers.

The biography, the members, and the web links need online lookups turned on. The library parts (what you own, your favorites, your totals, your tags) work whether or not they are.

## Track

This tab has listening totals for the song and the album, the file's format, and whether the track was decoded into memory or streamed because it was over the size cap. It also shows the ReplayGain being applied right now, worked out the same way playback does it, with a **Source** row that says where the gain came from: **from file**, **measured — staged**, or **measured — not in file**. Your tags for it sit at the bottom.

## Related

The rest of this album comes first, then looser connections: shared genre, year, or folder. Double-click to queue. Nothing here interrupts what's already playing.

## Playing

**Up next** is the queue. Drag to reorder, and double-click to jump. Clear removes what you queued by hand, not the rest behind it.

**Trail** is your last 500 plays, skips included. Double-click to hear something again, and playback returns to where it was.
