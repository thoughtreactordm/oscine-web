Curate is where playlists live, next to Discover — shelves of things to play next, drawn only from your library.

## Playlists

Create a playlist, drop tracks in, reorder them. The same song can appear twice. Export to `.m3u8` when you need the list somewhere else.

**My Favorites** sits at the top of the rail. It is a live view over the songs you have hearted, so you cannot delete it by accident the way you can a playlist.

Star a playlist or an artist to pin it in the Quick Menu on Now Playing, within reach while a record is on the Stage.

## Discover

Discover reads your files and your listening log and builds shelves from what it finds. There are nine recipes, each with its own angle: **Built for you** and **Deeper into** an artist, **Almost finished** and **Worth revisiting**, **Forgotten favorites** and **Because you favorited** an artist, **Guest appearances**, **Sitting unplayed**, and a **genre you own and ignore** shelf. Each card carries a one-line reason for being there.

<!-- shot: curate-discover -->

A shelf stays a shelf until you save it as a playlist, and nothing here leaves your machine.

## The queue

Up next has two layers. On top is what you queued by hand; below it, the rest of the session you started playing. Shuffle reorders the session, never the tracks you put up by hand. Clearing removes your hand-queued tracks and leaves the session behind them. The queue is for this sitting; playlists are what you keep.
