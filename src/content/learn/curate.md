Curate is where your playlists live, next to Discover's shelves of things to play next, all drawn from your own library.

## Playlists

Create a playlist, drop tracks in, and reorder them by dragging. The same song can appear twice. Dragging to reorder works in the plain list, not in **By album** view. Export to `.m3u8` when you need the list somewhere else. Right-click a playlist to give it its own [EQ preset](/learn/equalizer).

**Discover** sits at the top of the rail, with **My Favorites** right below it. My Favorites is a live view of the songs you've hearted, so you can't delete it by accident the way you can a playlist.

Star a playlist or an artist to pin it in the Quick Menu on Now Playing, so it's within reach while a record is on the Stage.

## Discover

Discover reads your files and your listening log and builds shelves from what it finds. There are ten recipes, each with its own angle: **Built for you** and **Deeper into** an artist, **Almost finished** and **Worth revisiting**, **Forgotten favorites** and **Because you favorited** an artist, **Guest appearances**, **Sitting unplayed**, a **genre you own and ignore** shelf, and **Tonight's crate**, a genre from your library picked fresh each day. Each card carries a one-line reason for being there.

<!-- shot: curate-discover -->

A shelf stays a shelf until you save it as a playlist, and nothing here leaves your machine.

## The queue

Up next has two layers. On top is what you queued by hand, and below it is the rest of what you started playing. Shuffle reorders that second layer, never the tracks you queued yourself. Clearing removes your hand-queued tracks and leaves the rest behind them.

By default the queue lasts for one sitting, and playlists are what you keep. Turn on **Keep the play queue between sessions** and Oscine brings the queue back, paused, the next time you open it.
