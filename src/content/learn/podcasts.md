Podcasts are their own tab. A show is a feed you subscribed to, not a folder Oscine scanned. Episodes aren't mixed into the music library, search, or ReplayGain.

<!-- shot: podcasts -->

## Subscribe

Paste a feed URL, bring your shows over from another app with **Import OPML**, or use Discover to browse Apple's catalog. Only the catalog needs **Allow online lookups**. Once you've subscribed to a show, Oscine refreshes its feed whether lookups are on or off, since you chose that address yourself.

## Download, then play

Oscine downloads an episode and plays the file. It doesn't stream it. Each episode has one button that downloads it, cancels while the download runs, and plays it once it's ready. You can also mark an episode played or unplayed by hand, or remove its download.

Per show, you can auto-download new episodes and keep the newest few (three by default, up to 50). Episodes you downloaded by hand are never pruned.

## Keeping up

An episode is marked played as soon as you start it, and you can flip any episode back to unplayed. Visiting a show refreshes it, at most once every 15 minutes, so the list stays current without hammering the feed.
